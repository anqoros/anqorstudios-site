Why this exists

The UAE gives fintech founders three different rulebooks. Nobody explains which one applies to them.

A fintech founder setting up in the UAE is choosing between mainland, DIFC, ADGM, and (for anything touching crypto) VARA, each with different licensing paths, banking relationships, and compliance obligations. Most of that knowledge lives in the heads of a small number of lawyers, compliance officers, and founders who've already been through it. This show puts that knowledge on the record.

For fintech founders

Direct, specific answers on licensing, banking, and compliance from people who've actually navigated it, not another generic "UAE is fintech-friendly" panel.

For the ecosystem

Visibility into which lawyers, compliance officers, and banks actually know what they're doing: a real reference, not a directory listing.

For Anqor

The same production model as Off Plan, proving the format extends across verticals, and putting Anqor in the room with the fintech vertical's actual decision-makers.
