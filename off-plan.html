Why this exists

Dubai off-plan has a trust problem. This is how we help close it.

Off-plan buyers in Dubai are cautious for real reasons: handover delays, escalating service charges, and launch-price buyers later undercut by better deals on the same building. Agents are paid by developers on commission, which is exactly why an independent, on-camera conversation with the people actually building these projects is worth more than another brochure.

For developers

A direct, credible channel to answer the delay and delivery questions buyers are already asking privately, before a broker has to answer for them.

For buyers & investors

Straight answers on track record, payment plans, and handover timelines from the source, not a sales script.

For Anqor

Ongoing, produced proof that we run a real video production house, and a natural feed into the Real Estate Intelligence Toolkit's developer-track-record data.

Season one · eight episodes

The full buyer journey, one pain point per episode.

Each episode targets a specific fear a real buyer already has, and points at exactly the kind of guest who can answer it credibly.

The Handover Truth
