Why we make them

The pipeline had to work on us first.

It's easy to sell video production. It's harder to sustain a weekly cadence with a small team, which is the thing clients actually need and the thing most agencies quietly can't do. So we built the pipeline — strategy, shoot, edit, and automated short-form repurposing through AnqorClip — and pointed it at our own shows before pointing it at anyone else's brand.

Each series targets a specific set of questions the audience already has and nobody answers publicly: handover risk in off-plan property, licensing and banking reality for fintech founders, and the operational truth of running hospitality in Dubai.
