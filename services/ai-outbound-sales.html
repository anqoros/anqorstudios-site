What this is

AI Outbound Sales, in practice.

Outbound breaks in predictable places: the list goes stale, the personalisation is obviously templated, and replies sit unread in a shared inbox until the intent has cooled. Each of those is an automation problem, not a headcount problem.

Outlia is our own outbound engine — multi-tenant, running in production, and used on Anqor's own pipeline. It handles discovery and enrichment, writes outreach that references something real about the prospect, watches for replies, and flags the ones worth a human's time.

We're deliberate about volume. Blasting a bad list faster is how domains get burned. The system is built to send less and land more.

What you get

Concretely, what's delivered.

  • Prospect discovery and enrichment against your actual ICP, refreshed rather than bought once
  • Personalised sequences that reference something specific, not a merge field
  • Reply monitoring with intent classification, so hot leads surface immediately
  • Human escalation at the moment of interest, with full thread context
  • Deliverability safeguards — sending limits, warmup, and domain protection
  • Pipeline reporting you can actually act on, not a vanity dashboard

Questions

Straight answers.

Isn't this just another cold-email tool?

The difference is that it's ours, running on our own pipeline, so it gets changed when it needs changing. It also monitors and classifies replies rather than stopping at send — which is where most tools hand the problem back to you.

How do you protect our sending domain?

Warmup, hard sending limits, and separate sending domains from your primary. We'd rather send fewer, better-targeted messages than optimise for volume and lose the domain.

Can it work alongside our existing CRM?

Yes. We build around what you already run rather than asking you to migrate first — that's the same approach we take on every system.
