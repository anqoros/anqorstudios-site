The problem

Outbound doesn't fail at send. It fails at everything around send.

Almost every outbound programme we're asked to fix is breaking in the same three places, and none of them are the email copy. The list goes stale within weeks. The personalisation is obviously a merge field, which prospects read as spam faster than any filter does. And replies land in a shared inbox where they sit until the intent has cooled.

The tooling market has optimised almost entirely for the send step, which is the one part that was never hard. What's hard is knowing who to contact this week rather than last quarter, writing something that references a real fact about their business, and reacting to a reply within the window where it still matters.

There's also a discipline problem the tools actively encourage. Sending more is the easiest lever to pull and the most expensive one to get wrong — burn a domain and you're not just paused, you're rebuilding sender reputation for months while your pipeline runs dry.

We build for the opposite default: send less, target harder, and put the engineering effort into discovery, enrichment and reply handling rather than throughput.

How it works

How it works, step by step.

Define the actual ICP

Not a job title and a company size. The specific, checkable signals that indicate someone has the problem you solve right now — hiring patterns, tech in use, funding events, expansion signals.

Discovery and enrichment

Prospects sourced and enriched against those signals continuously, so the list reflects this week rather than whenever it was bought.

Personalisation that references something real

Each message references a specific, verifiable fact about that company. This is where most automation gives itself away, and where the model earns its cost.

Controlled sending

Hard volume limits, warmed domains separated from your primary, and staggered sending. Deliberately slower than the tools allow.

Reply monitoring and classification

Every reply read and classified — interested, not now, wrong person, unsubscribe. Nothing sits unread waiting for someone to check a shared inbox.

Escalation at the moment of interest

A positive reply routes to a human immediately with the full thread and the prospect's context attached, while they're still at their desk.

What it isn't

Four things people assume this is.

A bigger send button

Volume is the lever most likely to destroy the asset you're building. We cap sending deliberately, and we'll push back if you ask us to raise it past what the domain can carry.

Scraped lists on autopilot

A list bought once and mailed forever is how you end up with 40% bounce rates. Discovery runs continuously against live signals.

Fully hands-off revenue

It gets a qualified, interested human to your sales team. Someone still has to close, and anyone selling you otherwise is selling a story.

A tool you have to operate

We run it or we hand it over trained. It isn't another dashboard for somebody on your team to babysit between other jobs.

Honest limits

What this won't do.

  • It will not fix a weak offer. If the message isn't landing because the proposition isn't compelling, more targeting won't rescue it — and we'll say so rather than take the retainer.
  • It needs a real ICP. 'Any business in Dubai' isn't targetable, and building the definition properly is the first week of work.
  • Open rates are not a metric we optimise. Since Apple Mail privacy protection they're substantially fiction. We report replies and meetings.
  • Results are not immediate. Domain warmup alone takes weeks before meaningful volume is safe.
Questions

Straight answers.

Isn't this just another cold-email tool?

The difference is what happens after send. Most tools stop at delivery and hand the reply problem back to you. This classifies every reply and escalates interest in real time. It's also our own product running on our own pipeline, so it gets fixed when it's wrong.

How do you protect our sending domain?

Separate sending domains from your primary, proper warmup, hard volume caps, and monitoring. We deliberately send below what the tooling would permit — a burned domain costs more than a slow month.

Can it work alongside our existing CRM?

Yes. We build around what you run rather than asking you to migrate first.

What volume should we expect?

Lower than a volume-first tool would promise, and with a materially better reply rate. We'll model it against your ICP size before you commit — if your total addressable list is 200 companies, outbound automation may be the wrong shape entirely and manual outreach is better.

Is cold outreach legal in the UAE?

Business-to-business outreach is generally permissible, subject to consent and unsubscribe requirements that vary by jurisdiction and by where your recipients are. We build unsubscribe handling and suppression in as standard, and we'd point you to counsel for a definitive position on your specific list.

Who writes the messaging?

We draft it, you approve it, and the system personalises within that approved frame. It doesn't improvise positioning.
