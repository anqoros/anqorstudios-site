What this is

Web Development, in practice.

Most agency websites are a template with the client's logo dropped in, and it shows in the load time, the markup and the search performance. We build custom — which sounds like a luxury and is mostly just a decision about where the effort goes.

Structure comes first: semantic markup, correct heading hierarchy, real structured data, fast images, and a URL architecture that makes sense to a crawler. Then design on top of that, rather than the reverse.

This site is the proof. It's custom-built, it carries the schema we're describing, and its own AI-search score is published on the site.

What you get

Concretely, what's delivered.

  • Custom design and build — no page builder, no purchased template
  • Structured data from the start, not retrofitted
  • Performance budget — compressed assets, no unnecessary framework weight
  • Responsive and accessible, verified at real breakpoints rather than assumed
  • A CMS if you need one, so your team can publish without a developer
  • Analytics and Search Console wired up properly at launch

Questions

Straight answers.

Do we get a CMS we can edit ourselves?

Yes, where it makes sense. For a brochure site it's often unnecessary overhead; for anything with listings, posts or a team roster it's essential. We'll be straight about which one you are.

Who owns the code?

You do, on handover. That's in writing.

Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. If you have a brand system we build to it. If you don't, we can build one — that's a separate conversation with its own scope.
