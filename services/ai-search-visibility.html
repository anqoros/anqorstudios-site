What this is

AI Search Visibility (GEO), in practice.

Generative Engine Optimisation is about being the source an assistant reaches for and cites. That depends on machine-readable structure, unambiguous entity data, genuinely specific content, and — bluntly — not blocking the crawlers in your robots.txt.

That last point is not hypothetical. We routinely find UAE agencies selling AI services whose own robots.txt blocks ClaudeBot, GPTBot and Google-Extended outright. They cannot be cited by the assistants their clients are asking about.

We run Ranqr on our own site first. The audit history is public and the numbers are real — this site's own GEO score moved from 42 to 75, which is the proof we'd rather point at than a testimonial.

What you get

Concretely, what's delivered.

  • Structured data — Organization, Person, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb, done properly

Questions

Straight answers.

Is GEO just SEO with a new name?

No, though they overlap. Classical SEO optimises for a ranked list of links. GEO optimises for being the source a model quotes in a synthesised answer, where there is no list and often only one or two citations. Structure and specificity matter far more; keyword density matters far less.

Can you prove it works?

On our own site, yes — the GEO score went from 42 to 75 and the audit log is on the site. For client work we measure the same way, from a baseline taken before anything changes.

What's the most common mistake you find?

Blocking the AI crawlers by accident, usually through a CDN's managed rules rather than a deliberate choice. It's the first thing we check and it's wrong surprisingly often — including on the sites of agencies selling AI services.
