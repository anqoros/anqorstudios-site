Before You Start

How to use this playbook

This is not a list of AI tools. It is a list of AI systems, custom-built workflows that replace specific, repeatable business tasks.

Every system described here has been built and deployed in a real business. Each section covers what the system does, who it makes sense for, how it works step by step, and the signal that tells you it is time to build one.

The rule: do not build an AI system for a problem you have not already solved manually. If you cannot describe the exact steps a human takes to do this today, you are not ready to automate it yet.

Start with the system that addresses your biggest bottleneck. That is almost always one of the first two: outbound sales or inbound qualification. Once one system is running, the next is easier, your team already understands how to work alongside AI.

Sales & Growth
AI Outbound Sales System

Who It Is For
  • B2B businesses doing outbound sales
  • Agencies looking to grow their client base
  • Founders who are personally doing all the outreach
  • Sales teams spending more time on research than conversations
How It Works
1 Define ICP
→
2 Prospect discovery
→
3 Research & enrichment
→
4 Message written
→
5 Sent & tracked
→
6 Reply monitored
→
7 Human takes over
⚡
Build this when: your team spends more than a few hours per week on manual prospecting, writing outreach, or following up with leads that went cold. Or when a founder is the only person doing sales and cannot scale that personally.
System 02 of 07
Inbound & Qualification
AI Inbound Lead Qualifier & Setter
What It Does
Responds to every inbound lead the moment they come in, via WhatsApp, email, or web form, qualifies them using your criteria, handles objections using your playbook, and books a confirmed meeting on your calendar. No lead sits unanswered.
Who It Is For
  • Any business with inbound enquiries
  • Service businesses where speed-to-respond wins deals
  • Businesses receiving enquiries outside business hours
  • Teams where the owner is the only one qualifying leads
How It Works
1 Lead arrives
→
2 Instant response
→
3 Qualification questions
→
4 Objection handled
→
5 Meeting booked
→
6 Human notified
⚡
Build this when: leads are waiting more than an hour for a first response, enquiries come in on weekends or evenings that nobody covers, or qualified and unqualified leads are both taking up the same amount of your time.

Operations & Compliance
Document & Compliance Automation
What It Does
Receives incoming documents (invoices, contracts, forms, filings), extracts the relevant data, validates it against your rules, generates the required compliant output, and routes it to the right system or recipient. No manual data entry. No document sits in an inbox waiting to be processed.
Who It Is For
  • Finance teams processing high invoice volumes
  • Businesses with regulatory filing requirements (UAE VAT, corporate filings)
  • Professional services handling contracts at volume
  • Any operation where humans are manually extracting data from documents
How It Works
1 Document received
→
2 Data extracted
→
3 Validated against rules
→
4 Compliant output generated
→
5 Routed and filed
⚡
Build this when: your team touches more than 15 to 20 documents per week that all follow a recognisable structure, errors are happening because of manual entry, or compliance deadlines are being missed because processing is slow.

System 05 of 07
Content & Media
Automated Video Clipping Engine
What It Does
Takes long-form video or audio (podcasts, webinars, interviews, sales calls), transcribes it, identifies the strongest moments based on topic, energy, and quotability, cuts clean clips, formats them for each platform, and outputs a folder of ready-to-publish shorts.
Who It Is For
  • Podcasters with long-form audio or video episodes
  • Content agencies repurposing client content at scale
  • Educators and coaches with webinar or course recordings
  • Businesses recording sales calls or product demos they are not repurposing
How It Works
1 Video uploaded
→
2 Transcribed
→
3 Highlights identified
→
4 Clips cut
→
5 Platform-formatted output
⚡
Build this when: you are producing long-form content regularly but not repurposing it into short clips, you have a backlog of recordings sitting unused, or a human editor is spending hours per video on cuts that follow a consistent pattern.
System 06 of 07
CRM & Sales Operations
CRM Follow-up Automation
What It Does
Monitors your CRM for deals that have gone quiet, drafts a contextually relevant follow-up based on the deal history, sends it (or queues it for approval), and logs the activity. No deal goes cold because someone forgot to follow up.
Who It Is For
  • Sales teams with more open deals than they can actively manage
  • Businesses where deals go cold and then die silently
  • High-ticket service providers with long sales cycles
  • Founders doing sales alone with no system keeping deals warm
How It Works
1 CRM scanned daily
→
2 Stale deals flagged
→
3 Context read
→
4 Follow-up drafted
→
5 Approved and sent
→
6 CRM updated
⚡
Build this when: you can look at your CRM and find deals that last had activity more than two weeks ago, follow-up is being done inconsistently across the team, or you know deals are dying because nobody is following up, not because the prospect said no.

Before You Build

How to prioritise

Do not build all seven. Build the one that addresses the most expensive bottleneck in your business right now. Here is the typical priority order for operator-led businesses.

Start here
Inbound Qualifier or Outbound Sales
Revenue is the constraint for most businesses. Fix the top of the funnel first. Everything else is easier to justify once revenue is moving.
Second
CRM Follow-up or WhatsApp System
Once leads are coming in, make sure none of them go cold or get lost. These systems protect the pipeline you just built.
Third
Document Automation or Reporting Agent
Operations start to crack when the business grows. Automate the back-office tasks that your team is doing manually before they become a drag on growth.
When ready
Video Clipping Engine
Build this when you have consistent long-form content and a clear distribution strategy. It multiplies what you already produce, it does not replace a content strategy.
