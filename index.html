Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does Anqor Studios actually do?

We build AI systems, software and content for operators in the UAE: inbound lead qualification, outbound sales automation, document and compliance automation, AI-native products, web and SaaS platforms, cloud and DevOps, security engineering, blockchain integration, AI search visibility, and video production. Most of it runs on products we built and operate on our own business first.

Where are you based?

Anqor Studios L.L.C-FZ is registered at Meydan Grandstand, 6th Floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, a Meydan Free Zone address. We work across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, and we're straightforward on our location pages about the fact that the office is in Dubai rather than listing addresses we don't hold.

How much does a build cost?

It depends on scope, and we quote properly rather than publish a number that would be wrong for most people. What we'll commit to is telling you on the first call if an off-the-shelf tool would do the job for less.

How long does a first system take?

Two to three weeks for a scoped first build, an inbound qualifier or a document automation flow live in your business, not a pilot or a slide deck. Larger multi-system rollouts take longer, but we'd rather get one thing working and earning first.

Who owns the code?

You do, on handover, in writing. That applies to every build.

Where does our data live?

UAE or EU regions depending on what your compliance position requires, and for sensitive workloads we can run models locally so data never leaves your infrastructure at all. The specific data-flow goes in writing before any build starts.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes, Arabic and English, including messages that switch between the two mid-thread, which is how a large share of UAE enquiries actually arrive. It's handled at the model layer rather than by translating an English response.

Do you only work with UAE businesses?

That's where we're based and where most of our work is, but the systems are remote-operated by design and we work with companies outside the UAE too.
