Questions

Abu Dhabi-specific questions, answered straight.

Do you have an office in Abu Dhabi?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Anqor Studios L.L.C-FZ is registered at Meydan Grandstand, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai. Abu Dhabi is about an hour and fifteen minutes away and we travel for on-site work — discovery, installation, training. Everything after that runs remotely, which is how these systems are meant to operate anyway.

Can you work with ADGM-regulated entities?

Yes. The relevant questions are where data sits and whether decisions are auditable, not where the vendor is registered. We deploy into your own cloud tenancy, keep model inference local for sensitive workloads, and log every automated decision so a compliance officer can reconstruct what happened and why.

What's a realistic timeline for a first working system?

Two to three weeks for a scoped first build — an inbound qualifier or a document flow live in your business, not a slide deck. Larger rollouts take longer, but we'd rather get one thing working and earning than spend a quarter on discovery.

Is there a minimum project size?

We're a studio, not an enterprise consultancy, so we're not carrying overhead that forces a six-figure floor. We'll say honestly on the first call if what you need is small enough that an off-the-shelf tool would serve you better.

Do you support Arabic-language workflows?

Yes — Arabic and English, including mixed-language threads. That matters more in Abu Dhabi than in some Dubai verticals, and it's handled at the model layer rather than bolted on with translation.
