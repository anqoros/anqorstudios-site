Questions

Sharjah-specific questions, answered straight.

Do you have an office in Sharjah?

No. Anqor Studios L.L.C-FZ is registered at Meydan Grandstand, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai. Most of Sharjah is a twenty-five to forty minute drive and we come out for discovery and installation. We'd rather be straight about that than list a Sharjah address we don't hold.

We're a small team — is AI automation realistic at our size?

Usually more realistic than at a large one, because there's less process to unpick. The systems that pay for themselves fastest in an SME are the boring ones: answering inbound enquiries out of hours, and getting documents out of inboxes and into whatever system you already use.

Can you work with our existing free zone licence and setup?

Yes — nothing we build depends on your licensing structure. What matters is which tools you already run: WhatsApp Business, a shared inbox, an accounting package, a CRM if you have one. We build around what exists rather than asking you to migrate first.

What does this actually cost for a Sharjah SME?

It depends on scope and we'll quote properly rather than guess here. What we will commit to is saying on the first call if an off-the-shelf tool would do the job for less — we'd rather not sell a custom build that isn't warranted.

Do you handle Arabic for customer-facing automation?

Yes, Arabic and English, including messages that switch between the two mid-thread. For a lot of Sharjah trading and manufacturing businesses that's the default way customers write, so it isn't optional.
