Context

What we've learned building for real estate.

This is the vertical we've built the most for, and the one where the gap between a good system and a contact form is most measurable. Dubai property enquiries are high-value, high-velocity and overwhelmingly mobile — the broker who replies in ten seconds is in a materially different position from the one who replies on Tuesday.

Beyond lead handling, we've built a full Real Estate Intelligence Engine: a brokerage website with listings and agent directory, an AI receptionist, and a market-intelligence layer covering handover delays by developer, service-charge trends by building, and price-per-sqft by area.

We also produce Off Plan, an on-camera interview series with Dubai developers, brokers and investors — because the questions buyers ask privately about handover risk are the ones nobody answers publicly.

The recurring problems

Where the money leaks.

Enquiries die in the gap

A WhatsApp enquiry at 10pm gets answered at 10am, by which time the buyer has messaged three other brokerages. An always-on qualifier closes that gap entirely.

Agents can't be reached in real time

Prospects want a specific agent who knows a specific tower. A routed directory with live availability beats a general contact form that lands nowhere.

Buyers ask questions brochures don't answer

Handover delays, service charges, launch-price risk. Answering those with real data closes faster than answering them with a developer's marketing PDF.

Questions

Real Estate-specific questions.

Can the system match enquiries to live listings?

Yes — that's the core of it. An enquiry gets parsed for budget, bedrooms, area and intent, matched against current inventory, and routed to the agent who holds that stock, with the matched listings already in the reply.

Do you work with off-plan as well as resale?

Both. Off-plan has extra requirements — payment plans, handover dates, developer track record — which is exactly what the market-intelligence layer is for.

Can it handle Arabic enquiries?

Yes, Arabic and English including mixed threads, which is a large share of real Dubai enquiry traffic.
